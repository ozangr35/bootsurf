Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Who we are

BootSurf (Netherlands) operates Euno, a privacy-first wellness journal and optional AI coach for iOS. Contact support@euno.app.

Journal data stays on your device

Your journal is device-local. Entries are stored on your iPhone, not on Euno servers.

Optional HealthKit

If you choose to connect HealthKit, Euno may read the health data you allow so you can reflect on it in the app. This is optional and controlled in iOS Settings.

Optional AI

AI coaching is optional. Before a prompt is sent, Euno scrubs personally identifiable information on-device. Requests go through a proxy that does not store your journal.

Rate limiting

A Keychain UUID is used only to rate-limit AI requests. It is not used to build a profile or sell data.

Notifications

Reminders use local notifications on your device. They are not sent from a remote server.

Export and delete

You can export or delete your journal in Settings. Deleting your data in the app removes it from the device.

Contact

Questions: support@euno.app.
